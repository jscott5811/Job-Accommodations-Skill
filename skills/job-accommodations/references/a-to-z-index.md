# JAN A-to-Z Index

The complete index of JAN's A-to-Z of Disabilities and Accommodations, as listed at
https://askjan.org/a-to-z.cfm (verified 2026-10-06).

**How JAN's A-to-Z works:** JAN's A-to-Z is a starting point in the accommodation process and
may not address every situation. Accommodations are made case by case, considering each
employee's individual limitations and accommodation needs. Employers and individuals are
encouraged to contact JAN ((800) 526-7234) to discuss specific situations. Entry pages
contain ADA information, accommodation ideas, and resources for additional information.

**How to use this index inside the skill:** When the user's condition, limitation, or topic
appears below, open its JAN page via the A-to-Z page (or the deep links given for the most
commonly used entries). Entry pages follow a consistent structure: definition/ADA notes,
accommodation ideas, real customer scenarios, and JAN's 9-step accommodation checklist.
Quote accommodation ideas from the page — do not invent ones the page does not list.

## Notes for the skill

- The per-disability accommodation ideas live in `references/accommodations-by-disability/` (`a-b.md`, `c-d.md`, `e-g.md`, `h-p.md`, `q-z.md`): all 103 JAN disability pages, extracted 2026-10-06, each section organized by limitation with its source URL and real-case examples where JAN gives them. 13 disabilities have no accommodation list on their JAN pages (Fetal Alcohol Syndrome, Learning Disability, Lupus, Lyme Disease, Marfan Syndrome, Anxiety Disorder, Bipolar Disorder, Depression, Eating Disorders, OCD, Personality Disorder, Phobias, Schizophrenia) — those pages point to JAN's "Accommodation Solutions: Executive Functioning Deficits" publication instead, fully extracted in `references/executive-functioning-deficits.md`.
- Every entry page carries JAN's contact options: (800) 526-7234 (Voice), TTY
  (304) 293-7186, jan@askjan.org, and Employer Live Chat.
- JAN states its A-to-Z PDFs are updated periodically (the accommodation PDFs were dated
  "Updated 06/01/2026" at last check). If suggesting an accommodation from an entry page,
  confirm it is still listed — do not quote from memory alone.
- The Situations and Solutions Finder (https://askjan.org/sitsol/index.cfm) is the
  companion search: it finds real employer scenarios by disability, limitation, or
  occupation. Use it alongside the A-to-Z when the user's job type matters.
